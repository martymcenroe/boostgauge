### 2.5 Logic Flow (Pseudocode)

```
1. In render_face() during bezel ring drawing:
   - Calculate gradient stops for the ring's radial sweep.
   - Insert a sharp drop to channel mean < 100 near 1.11 R.
   - Insert an immediate return to channel mean > 240 within 0.02 R of the drop.
2. In test_bezel_ring_horizon_returns_to_bright():
   - Render the static face at size 1024.
   - Sample pixels at math angles 90° and 180° on a 0.005 R grid from [1.05 R, 1.24 R].
   - Find r_d, the smallest sampled radius with mean < 100.
   - Assert r_d is found and <= 1.18 R.
   - Assert at least one sample in (r_d, r_d + 0.02 R] has mean > 240.
```

### 2.6 Technical Approach

* **Module:** `src/boostgauge/skins/stingray.py`, `tests/visual/test_stingray_static.py`
* **Pattern:** Declarative visual assertions over deterministic rendering
* **Key Decisions:**
  - Adjusting the `render_face` gradient interpolation precisely to compress the ramp into a band that recovers to > 240 intensity. The existing shipped render tops at 190, failing the specification.
  - Adding a high-resolution 0.005 R grid test dedicated to this measurement, leaving the existing 2px walk regression suite unmodified, as mandated by the rulings.

### 2.7 Architecture Decisions

| Decision | Options Considered | Choice | Rationale |
|----------|-------------------|--------|-----------|

**Architectural Constraints:**
- The existing #379 visual assertions must not be modified and must pass against the updated render.
