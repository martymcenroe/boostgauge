## 3. Requirements

1. The app shall instantiate four Telltale instances: three with durations read from config keys (telltale_windows.short, medium, long) and one with window None for all-time.
2. The app shall feed every collector sample to all four instances via Telltale.update(timestamp, value), each receiving identical timestamp and value arguments.
3. The app shall pass all four current_peak() values to the renderer on every refresh, passing a None peak unmodified through the wiring layer.
4. The short-window menu entry shall call reset() on the short-window instance; the remaining three must be untouched.
5. The medium-window menu entry shall call reset() on the medium-window instance; the remaining three must be untouched.
6. The long-window menu entry shall call reset() on the long-window instance; the remaining three must be untouched.
7. The all-time menu entry shall call reset() on the all-time instance; the remaining three must be untouched.
8. The 'Reset All' menu entry shall call reset() on all four instances.
9. Menu entry labels shall derive from one pure formatter over the configured duration ('Nh', 'Nm', 'Ns', 'All-time'), outputting 'Reset ' plus the label.
10. The tooltip text function shall be pure and identify the four windows by color ('<window label> — cyan', etc.), mapped respectively to cyan, orange, magenta, coral red.
11. Hovering the gauge shall display a tooltip containing the four formatted identification lines.
12. Render-tier tests shall assert the composed image produced through the real renderer via pixel classification against cyan `#3BD7F0`, orange `#FF9A2E`, magenta `#D45BE8`, and coral red `#FF6E7A`.
13. A render-tier post-reset case shall send no samples between reset and capture to assert the absence of the reset needle's color family.
